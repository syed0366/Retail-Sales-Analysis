# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Findings

## Overview

The purpose of Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was to understand sales patterns, customer behavior, product performance, and overall business trends within the retail sales dataset.

---

## 1. Revenue by Product Category

### Objective

Identify which product categories generate the highest revenue.

### Findings

* Electronics generated the highest revenue.
* Clothing followed closely behind Electronics.
* Beauty contributed the lowest revenue among the three categories.

### Insight

Electronics is the strongest revenue-driving category and should remain a key business focus.

---

## 2. Quantity Sold by Product Category

### Objective

Analyze sales volume across categories.

### Findings

* Clothing recorded the highest number of units sold.
* Electronics ranked second.
* Beauty sold the fewest units.

### Insight

Although Clothing generates slightly lower revenue than Electronics, it drives the highest sales volume.

---

## 3. Revenue by Gender

### Objective

Compare revenue contribution between male and female customers.

### Findings

* Revenue contribution from Male and Female customers was nearly balanced.
* No significant dominance was observed from either gender.

### Insight

Marketing campaigns can be designed for both customer groups without heavy bias.

---

## 4. Revenue by Age Group

### Objective

Understand which age groups contribute the most revenue.

### Findings

* Customers aged 50+ generated the highest revenue.
* Age groups 20–29, 30–39, and 40–49 contributed similar revenue levels.
* Customers below 20 generated the lowest revenue.

### Insight

Older customers represent an important revenue-generating segment.

---

## 5. Average Order Value by Category

### Objective

Measure average transaction value across product categories.

### Findings

* Beauty products achieved the highest Average Order Value.
* Electronics ranked second.
* Clothing had the lowest Average Order Value.

### Insight

Premium product offerings in Beauty can help maximize revenue growth.

---

## Key EDA Insights

* Electronics generated the highest revenue.
* Clothing achieved the highest sales volume.
* Beauty recorded the highest Average Order Value.
* Revenue contribution between genders remained balanced.
* Customers aged 50+ contributed the highest revenue share.

---

## Conclusion

The exploratory analysis revealed important trends in customer behavior and product performance. Electronics remains the top revenue-generating category, while Clothing drives the highest sales volume. Customer demographics, particularly age groups, significantly influence purchasing behavior and provide opportunities for targeted business strategies.
